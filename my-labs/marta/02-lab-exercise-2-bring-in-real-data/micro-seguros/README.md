# Exercise 2 · Bring in real data

A prototype feels real when it shows real things: tomorrow's wind at the beach, the vets near where a dog was last seen, the neighbourhood a sofa is in. Lots of this data is free and needs no account.

Your project's page is in your folder (open it in **Preview**). It's a real screen, but the grey dashed boxes are **not connected yet**: that's where real data could go. You don't write the code: ask the AI agent, in your own words.

Your cheat sheet is in `cheat-sheets/`. You don't need to read it all: ask the AI agent about it.

## Think like a designer

- **What's worth showing?** One piece of data a person cares about beats ten they don't.
- **What if it's slow, or fails?** The person should still be able to carry on, calmly.
- **Whose data is it?** Show areas, not home addresses.
