## Behaviour and states

- Bottom sheet over the active session. Title "Extend your session", "No gap in cover. Same price per minute."
- Chips pick the extra time (+30 min selected); rows update: "New end time 19:50", "Extra cost + €X.XX".
- **Confirm** (+ €X.XX) extends and closes the sheet → [[08.1 Active session]] with the new end time.
- **Cancel** or tapping the dimmed background closes the sheet.

## Data

New end time and extra cost from the back end. Does extending need a new payment confirmation? ==to decide==

## Edge cases

- Extending past the 4 h max: disable the chips that go over ==to confirm==.
- The countdown keeps running behind the sheet.
