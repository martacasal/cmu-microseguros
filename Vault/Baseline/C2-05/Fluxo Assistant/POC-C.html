POC C

A frase

The homepage is one sentence you can read out loud: "Vou correr durante 45 min, sem proteger o equipamento." It comes pre-filled from your last activity; every underlined word is a tap target that opens a small picker. The quote is a review card you approve or reject, and the activity screen is a timeline that logs everything the system did.

Behaviour

  • Guided 5/0: the sentence is the whole step; one button forward.
  • Proactive 3/2: pre-filled from last time; near the end it suggests the extension it thinks you need, and you still decide.
  • Minimal 3/2: a single sentence; the quote card lists each line with its reason.
  • Direct 4/1: tap words and pick; the sentence gives a light conversational feel (the 1 point of intent-based) without typing.
  • Review 2/3: the quote is an Approve / Reject card and the timeline is an automatic log, but every value stays user-set.
  • Staged 4/1: price after Começar; removing equipment marks the total as updated; extend needs Confirmar.
