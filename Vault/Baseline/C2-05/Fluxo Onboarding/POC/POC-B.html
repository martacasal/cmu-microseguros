POC B

Conversa guiada

The whole onboarding is one conversation, so the first impression is meeting Pulse. On Welcome, Pulse introduces itself. The 3 value points are its first messages, account creation (Apple / Google / email + code) happens inside the chat, and the 12 questions continue in the same thread. Once the account exists, the start of the conversation folds into a "Conta criada ✓" line. The 12 questions become a calm, one-way conversation with Pulse. Each question arrives as a message that first explains why it is asked; the answer controls (chips, fields, checkboxes) sit in the composer at the bottom, and each answer stays in the thread as a record the user can read back.

How it follows the spectrum

  • Guided 5/0: the thread only moves forward; Back takes the last answer out of the thread and asks again, with the answer kept.
  • Proactive 3/2: Pulse explains before asking and suggests from earlier answers; it never interrupts.
  • Minimal 4/1: one question at a time; legal detail sits in accordions inside the message.
  • Direct 3/2 + conversational 2: answers are still taps on chips, but "O que praticas?" also accepts a sentence ("corro e jogo padel").
  • Step control 4/1 + review 1: when Pulse reads a sentence, it proposes chips and waits for Confirm / Adjust.
  • On-commit 5/0: nothing is checked while typing; Pulse replies only after Send.
