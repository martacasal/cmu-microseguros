## Behaviour and states

- Title "How do you want to pay?". Options: **MB WAY** (selected by default), Card, Apple Pay / Google Pay [V].
- With MB WAY selected, the "MB WAY mobile" field shows (+351 9XX XXX XXX).
- "Session total €X.XX" row, insurer seal, then **Pay and turn on** (€X.XX).
- MB WAY → [[07.2 Waiting for MB WAY]]. Card and Apple/Google Pay flows ==not designed==.

## Data

Session total, saved payment method and mobile number if the user has them. The payment request goes to the MB WAY provider.

## Edge cases

- Invalid phone number: inline error on the field, CTA disabled ==to confirm==.
- Apple Pay / Google Pay only shown when the device supports it.
