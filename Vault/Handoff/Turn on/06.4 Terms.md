## Behaviour and states

- Title "Terms, in plain English". Seal: "Insurance issued by [Partner insurer], supervised by ASF."
- Five FAQ items expand on tap (+): Why am I not covered today? (shown expanded: "The law only requires it for federations, gyms and events."), Who backs this insurance?, How does per-minute cover work?, What do I do if I get hurt?, Can I cancel? Refunds? [V].
- **Open full document (PDF)** opens the policy PDF.
- Back returns to [[06.1 Quote]].

Insurer name, FAQ copy, policy PDF URL. If the PDF fails to load, ==not designed yet==.
