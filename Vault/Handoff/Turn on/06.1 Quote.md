## Behaviour and states

- Overline: "FOOTBALL · TODAY, 18:00 TO 19:20". Big price "€X.XX" and "for 1h 20. You only pay for what you use."
- **Why this price**: three bars (Time, Medium risk, Gear), each with its share of the price.
- **✓ Covered** card (black): Tratamento, Invalidez, Equipamento, "up to [AMOUNT]".
- **✕ Not covered** card: "Outside your slot", "Federation events"; "See all ›" opens the full list ([[06.4 Terms]] ==to confirm==).
- Help (?) opens [[06.4 Terms]].
- **Pay and turn on** (€X.XX) goes to [[07.1 Pay]]. Note under it: "You're only charged when you confirm."
