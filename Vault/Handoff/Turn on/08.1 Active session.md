## Behaviour and states

- "Covered" pill (green dot) top left, **SOS** button top right.
- Time ring counts down: "52:14 left · ends at 19:20".
- "€X.XX so far, capped at €X.XX" updates as time passes.
- **Extend** chips (+15 min, +30 min selected, +1 h), then **Extend** → [[08.2 Extend]].
- **End**: press and hold → [[08.3 End]] ("Press and hold to end").
- At the end time, cover ends by itself → [[09.2 Ended automatically]].

## Data

Session end time, price per minute, cap. The countdown runs on the device but must stay in sync with the server.

## Edge cases

- App in background / phone locked: countdown keeps going; reminder 15 min before the end.
- SOS behaviour ==not designed== (call, claims?).
