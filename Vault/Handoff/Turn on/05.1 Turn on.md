## Behaviour and states

- Title shows the chosen activity and place: "Football at Parque da Cidade".
- **Activity tiles**: one selected at a time (black). "+ Other" opens [[05.2 Other activity]].
- **Risk badge** updates with the activity (low / medium / high, plus the body parts covered).
- **Duration**: − / + change it by 15 min; the bar under it shows one segment per 15 min (16 segments = 4 h). The subtitle shows the end time and the limit: "until 19:20 · max. 4 h [V]". − is disabled at 15 min, + at 4 h.
- **Cover my gear**: toggle; adds "+ €X.XX · ball and boots" (gear depends on the activity).
- **See quote** (shows the running price €X.XX) goes to [[05.4 Calculating]].
- Back button leaves the flow; help (?) opens help.

## Data

Activities and their risk level, place of the session, current time (to work out the end time), gear price for the activity. If the place is missing, ==not designed yet==.

## Edge cases

- Long place names in the title: wrap to two lines, don't truncate the activity.
- Session that crosses midnight: the end time must show the day.
- Activity with no gear to cover: hide the toggle row ==to confirm==.
