## Behaviour and states

- Title: "Which sport are you doing?". Search field with placeholder "e.g. basketball, climbing" filters the list as the user types.
- Each item shows icon, name and risk level (Basketball · Medium, Beach volleyball · Low, Surfing · High, Climbing · High).
- Tapping a covered sport selects it and returns to [[05.1 Turn on]] with it chosen.
- Sports not covered (Kitesurfing · "Not covered yet") show × and can't be picked.

## Data
