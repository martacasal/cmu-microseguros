## Behaviour and states

- No back button. Title "Confirm in the MB WAY app".
- Countdown card: "REQUEST EXPIRES IN 3:45", "We sent a €X.XX request." Counts down every second.
- Note: "Cover only starts once the payment is approved."
- When the payment is approved → [[07.3 Cover on]].
- **Cancel request** cancels the MB WAY request and returns to [[07.1 Pay]] ==to confirm==.

## Data

Payment status from the MB WAY provider (pending, approved, declined, expired).

## Edge cases

- Timer reaches 0:00: ==expired state not designed==.
- User declines in MB WAY: ==not designed==.
- User leaves and comes back: the timer must reflect the real time left.
