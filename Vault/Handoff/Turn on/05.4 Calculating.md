## Behaviour and states

- No back button: the user waits. Title "Preparing your quote", summary "Football · 1h 20 · with gear".
- Five steps run in order: Activity and level → Duration → Place → Gear → Price. Each one goes Next → **Now** (black badge, ring icon) → **Done** (check).
- The progress bar fills one segment per step; the counter shows "n of 5 done" and the time left ("about 5 s").
- When Price is done, goes automatically to [[06.1 Quote]].
- Footnote: "Your age and fitness don't affect the price [V]."

## Data

Each step is a back-end check (risk for the activity, duration, place, gear, price). If a step takes longer than expected or fails, ==not designed yet==.
