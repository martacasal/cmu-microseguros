POC final

Micro-seguro

Built from the group's feedback on POC A and POC B. POC C was dropped.

Passo 1 · Configurar

  • BOne screen: activity tiles, dial, equipment, step navigator at the top.
  • AThe activity's risk shows as soon as you pick it, before you start.
  • ADuration is preset per activity, and uses your own average when you have history.
  • new"Outro" opens a searchable list, and you can type any sport.
  • newDial goes up to 4 h.
  • A→toggleEquipment is a switch, not its own screen.

Passo 2–3 · Cálculo e preço

  • AChecklist loader that shows what is being calculated.
  • newNo dial on the quote: a dial with a handle you can't move was a false affordance. The price stands alone, with a split bar and expandable reasons.

Passo 4 · Atividade

  • BThe dial drains as the countdown; drag it forward to extend.
  • newSOS button that calls 112 or the 24 h assistance line.
  • AParar and Prolongar are plain buttons with a confirm step.
