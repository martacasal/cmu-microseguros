POC B

O mostrador

One object carries the whole flow: a dial. On the homepage you drag it to set how long you'll play. On the quote it holds the price. During the activity the same arc drains as the countdown, and you extend by dragging it forward again. Activity tiles and an equipment switch sit around it, all on one screen.

Behaviour

  • Guided 5/0: four fixed states of the same screen; no menus, no side paths.
  • Proactive 3/2: picking an activity moves the dial to its usual length; a gentle note near the end.
  • Minimal 3/2: one number in the dial; the "why" is a single stacked bar with three labelled parts.
  • Direct 4/1: drag the dial, flip the switch, hold to stop.
  • Step control 3/2: the user sets every value by hand.
  • Staged 4/1: price is not shown while dragging; it appears only after Começar. Extending shows the extra cost and waits for Confirmar.
